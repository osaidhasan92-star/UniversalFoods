V21:
The child submenu is now implemented with a dedicated third grid column.
When the mouse enters a parent such as Food Business Line, JavaScript copies
that parent's child links into the SAME mega-menu's right column.

Important:
- No absolute-positioned child popup.
- No nested floating dropdown.
- Left = section description.
- Middle = parent items.
- Right = selected parent's children.
- Moving from the parent into the right column keeps the children visible.
- Hovering another parent replaces the right-column content.
- Leaving the mega menu clears the right column.
- Mobile remains an accordion.
